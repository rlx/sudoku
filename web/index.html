Put Sudoku on your Home Screen

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
    (If you can't see it, first tap the menu button next to the web address.)
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.
  4. Close Safari and open Sudoku from your Home Screen.
Download the app
  1. Tap Download the app above.
  2. Open the downloaded file.
  3. If the phone asks, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If a "Play Protect" box appears, tap Install anyway.
